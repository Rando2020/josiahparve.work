## Residual risks

No residual high-risk item may be accepted until the named launch authority and relevant control owner review the evidence. Any temporary exception must include an owner, reason, expiration date, monitoring plan, and remediation commitment.

## Rollback position

Rollback cannot be relied upon until:

- A named decision owner accepts authority.
- The prior known-good package is identified.
- The recovery runbook is tested.
- Communication triggers and audiences are approved.

## Conditions required to change recommendation to Go

- All mandatory blockers closed
- Critical UAT scenarios passed
- Security and privacy dispositions recorded
- Data and delivery reconciliation approved
- Rollback rehearsal passed
- Residual risks accepted by named authority
- Customer and internal launch communication approved
