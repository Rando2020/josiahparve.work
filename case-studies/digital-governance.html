Case study 03

Building analytics governance that protected speed and trust.

A generalized case study based on enterprise digital analytics work across CVS and Aetna properties, covering measurement, validation, privacy, and cross-functional adoption.

The challenge

Measurement quality and privacy could not be inspected only after launch.

Digital properties needed consistent analytics instrumentation for attribution, product analytics, and personalization while also managing HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA, consent, and PHI exposure risk. Manual validation could not keep pace with the number of properties and releases.

The model

Move governance upstream and make specifications testable.

I partnered with engineering to create a validation framework that compared live pre-release pages against tagging specifications. This changed governance from a documentation exercise into a repeatable quality control embedded before production.

  • Standardized tagging specifications and business requirements
  • Connected measurement intent to implementation acceptance criteria
  • Automated pre-release validation for Adobe Analytics and Tealium deployments
  • Created a Confluence system of record for tagging, consent, and compliance
  • Audited and remediated privacy and PHI exposure gaps
  • Aligned product, marketing, analytics, engineering, and privacy stakeholders

Measured impact

Quality defects were caught before they became production data problems.

Why it matters now

The same operating pattern applies to AI-era product delivery.

Modern product teams are moving faster with AI-generated specifications, code, experiments, and workflows. The lesson remains the same: speed becomes valuable only when intent, instrumentation, validation, evidence, and accountable review remain connected.
