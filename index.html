My operating model

From ambiguity to accountable execution.

I treat implementation as a product system with explicit inputs, controls, evidence, ownership, and learning loops.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Clarify the customer, problem, constraints, success measures, and hidden assumptions.

  2. 02

    Define

    Normalize requirements, map stakeholders, and separate needs from requested solutions.

  3. 03

    Design

    Translate requirements into data flows, integration contracts, controls, and delivery plans.

  4. 04

    Build

    Structure the backlog, remove blockers, and keep product, engineering, and customers aligned.

  5. 05

    Validate

    Test the data, workflows, environments, permissions, exceptions, and user outcomes.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Run evidence-based readiness reviews with clear Go, No-Go, and rollback decisions.

  7. 07

    Stabilize

    Monitor adoption, incidents, support patterns, defects, and operational health.

  8. 08

    Improve

    Convert recurring friction into automation, standards, product feedback, and permanent fixes.
